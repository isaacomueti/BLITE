Our story

Food that connects generations.

Founded with passion and purpose, B-Lite Food was created to celebrate Nigerian culture through food. We're proud to serve both Nigerians abroad longing for a taste of home and food lovers eager to explore the bold, colourful world of Nigerian cuisine.

From our kitchen in Hatfield, Hertfordshire, we cook for everyday tables, for offices, and for the celebrations people remember — like Pauline's 85th birthday.

Because at B-Lite Food, we don't just cook — we care. We save you time, we lift your stress, and we remind you that good food should always be within reach.

Our vision

To become the leading Nigerian food experience in the UK — not just serving meals, but sharing a lifestyle rooted in culture, community and comfort.

We want every customer to feel closer to home, no matter where they are.

How we cook

Standards you can trust

Meals are prepared daily in a licensed, fully compliant kitchen by staff trained in UK food hygiene and safety.

Fresh, local ingredients

Ingredients are locally sourced and stored in temperature-controlled conditions, then cooked fresh to order.

Less waste
